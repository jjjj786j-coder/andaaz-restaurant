# Andaaz Restaurant — verification gate

**Status: Needs Verification**  
**Scope:** This file records the evidence and approvals still required before the website can be presented as an approved operating-business website. It does not claim that the restaurant, contact channels, menu, or photographs have been independently verified.

## Mandatory eligibility checks

| Requirement | Current state | Evidence still required |
|---|---|---|
| Identifiable operating business | Business name and Lahore address are present in the project, but not independently confirmed here | Current official business record or restaurant-supplied confirmation, plus official website/source |
| Working phone | A phone number is published in the project | Restaurant confirmation that the approved number reaches the business and is monitored |
| Working WhatsApp | A WhatsApp handoff number is present in the project | Restaurant-approved number and documented confirmation that messages are received and monitored |
| Domain email | An email is referenced in the existing project materials | Restaurant confirmation that the address is on the official domain and monitored |
| Current operations | Existing project materials describe dinner service | Recent official activity or other credible current operating evidence |
| Ordering, inquiry, or reservation process | The frontend can prepare handoff messages | Restaurant-confirmed process and a real endpoint or approved handoff destination |
| Current menu | Local menu records and PKR prices exist in the frontend | Current restaurant-approved menu, prices, portions, variants, and availability |

## Business information to confirm

Please obtain written confirmation of:

- official trading name and approved logo;
- exact address, branch wording, and map pin;
- approved phone, WhatsApp, and domain email;
- opening days and hours, including kitchen/order cutoff;
- dine-in, takeaway, delivery, and catering availability;
- delivery areas, minimum order, charges, taxes, service charges, and payment methods;
- reservation availability, deposit, cancellation, no-show, late-arrival, parking, and accessibility policies.

## Menu and allergen gate

The frontend must not publish a price or availability as current until the restaurant approves it. Missing ingredients, allergens, dietary labels, spice levels, and cross-contact information must remain undisclosed or state: **“Allergen information not provided. Please ask the restaurant.”**

## Photography and branding gate

The local image files are available for layout work, but their reuse permission and subject identity are not established by this repository alone. For every image, the restaurant must supply or approve:

- the actual dish, dining area, storefront, or event depicted;
- source or supplied filename;
- permission basis for website reuse;
- accurate caption and branch context;
- approved logo and any brand-use restrictions.

Unapproved or uncertain images must be removed, replaced with `assets/images/food-placeholder.svg`, or held until permission is documented. Public visibility is not permission.

## Feature-dependent information

The following are optional and should be omitted when unavailable: reviews and quotations, awards, dietary labels, catering details, event galleries, social links, and detailed ingredient lists. Their absence does not by itself disqualify the business; inventing them does.

The home footer now links Instagram (`https://www.instagram.com/andaazrestaurantlahore/`) and Facebook (`https://www.facebook.com/AndaazRestaurant`) because those destinations are exposed by the restaurant's official website footer. Social account ownership and ongoing monitoring should still be confirmed before launch.

## Release decision

Coding changes can be previewed, but production approval, Zanderio installation, and claims of eligibility remain paused until the mandatory items above and essential image permissions are documented. No calls, messages, or emails should be sent without authorization.
